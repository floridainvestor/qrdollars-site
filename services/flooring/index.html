Quality Results LLC installs luxury vinyl plank (LVP) and tile flooring for property owners and managers in Jacksonville and St. Johns County. Most of the flooring we install goes into rental units between tenants, so durability and turnaround time matter as much as how the floor looks the day we finish.

What's included

A flooring job starts with tear-out of whatever's there now — old carpet, sheet vinyl, or cracked tile — and haul-off of the debris. From there we check the subfloor for level and moisture, make repairs where needed, and install LVP or tile with the transition strips and thresholds needed at doorways and material changes.

Why LVP for rentals

Luxury vinyl plank has become the standard for rental turns because it's waterproof, resists scratches from move-in and move-out traffic, and doesn't need refinishing the way hardwood does. It also holds up better than laminate in bathrooms, laundry areas, and kitchens where water gets on the floor. Tile still gets used in wet areas and entryways where owners want a harder surface.

Florida slabs and subfloor prep

Most homes in the Jacksonville area are built on concrete slab foundations, and slab moisture is the single biggest cause of flooring failures we see — adhesive that won't hold, planks that cup, or tile that cracks. Before installing over a slab we check for moisture and level issues rather than assuming the old flooring was hiding a good subfloor. On older housing stock, subfloors can also be out of level from decades of settling, which adds leveling compound to the job before flooring goes down.

Timeline and scheduling

A single room typically installs in a day. A full unit — tear-out, subfloor prep, and install — usually runs two to three days depending on total square footage and how much of the old flooring needs to come out first.

What affects the price

  • Total square footage being installed
  • Material grade — LVP thickness and wear layer, or tile size and type
  • Tear-out and haul-off of existing flooring
  • Subfloor repair or leveling before install
  • Transitions, thresholds, and baseboard removal/reinstall
