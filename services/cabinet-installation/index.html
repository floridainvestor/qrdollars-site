Quality Results LLC installs kitchen and bath cabinet sets, hardware, and countertops for property owners and managers in Jacksonville and St. Johns County. This is cabinet installation work — setting new or replacement cabinet boxes, not refinishing or repainting existing cabinets in place.

What's included

A cabinet job covers removal of the old cabinets, layout and leveling of the new boxes against the actual wall condition, hanging upper cabinets, setting base cabinets, installing hardware, and setting the countertop once the boxes are secure. We coordinate cutouts and connections for sinks, dishwashers, and disposals so plumbing and electrical line up with the new layout instead of the old one.

Full kitchens and single vanities

Most of the cabinet work we do is a full kitchen — uppers, lowers, and countertop — as part of a unit turn or renovation. We also install single-vanity swaps in bathrooms, which is a much smaller job on its own. Either way, the walls get checked for level and square before cabinets go up, since older homes rarely have perfectly straight walls and shimming is part of a clean install.

Timeline and scheduling

A cabinet-only install — boxes and hardware, no countertop — usually takes one to two days. A full kitchen with countertops runs three to five days depending on the countertop material and how much plumbing and electrical coordination is needed for sink and appliance hookups.

What affects the price

  • Linear footage of upper and lower cabinets
  • Whether walls or soffits need modification to fit the new layout
  • Countertop material and edge profile
  • Plumbing and electrical coordination for sinks, dishwashers, and disposals
  • Hardware finish and style
