Quality Results LLC handles full make-ready turns for property owners and managers in Jacksonville and St. Johns County — the deep clean and detail work that gets a unit rent-ready between tenants. This is often the last step in a turn, after paint, flooring, or repairs are done, but it also runs standalone on units that just need a thorough clean before the next tenant moves in.

What's included

A make-ready turn covers the whole unit: appliance cleaning inside and out, cabinet interiors and exteriors, baseboards, light fixtures and switch plates, blinds, window tracks, and a full bathroom and kitchen deep clean. Floors get detailed last so nothing gets tracked back onto them. The goal is a unit that shows well on day one, not just a surface wipe-down.

Why this matters in Jacksonville's climate

Humidity is a real factor in how a vacant unit looks and smells by the time a new tenant walks through. A unit that's been closed up during a hot, humid stretch — especially in the summer months — can develop mustiness or mildew in bathrooms, closets, and under sinks even if nothing was visibly dirty when the last tenant left. Our turns include addressing that odor and any surface mildew, not just a standard wipe-down, so the unit doesn't smell closed-up during showings.

Timeline and scheduling

A single-unit turn clean typically runs same-day to one full day, depending on the size of the unit and how the previous tenant left it. Turns bundled with paint or flooring work get scheduled as the final step once that work is done and dust has settled.

What affects the price

  • Unit size and number of bathrooms/kitchens
  • Move-out condition — light cleaning versus heavy buildup or neglect
  • Whether the turn is bundled with paint or flooring work in the same visit
  • Odor or mildew treatment needed beyond a standard clean
